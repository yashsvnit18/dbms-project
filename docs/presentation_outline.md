# AI203 Presentation Outline

Suggested 9-slide presentation for an 8-10 minute walkthrough.

1. **Title and Team** - Hostel Management System, AI203, SVNIT Surat, team names and assigned roles.
2. **Problem and Objectives** - Hostel records to manage; goals of consistent allocation, payment tracking, and simple reporting.
3. **User Roles and Workflows** - Admin and student permissions; admission → allocation → fee payment; complaint and visitor flows.
4. **ER Diagram** - Present the Mermaid ER model from `technical_report.md` and explain the key one-to-many relationships.
5. **Relational Design and Normalization** - Nine relations, PK/FK/UNIQUE/CHECK constraints, concise UNF-to-3NF reasoning.
6. **SQL Demonstration** - Show one multi-table join, one aggregate with `HAVING`, and one subquery from `database/queries.sql`.
7. **Advanced DBMS Features** - Vacancy view, allocation procedure, occupancy trigger, transaction rollback case, and composite index with EXPLAIN.
8. **Application Demo** - Admin overview/CRUD and reports; student profile/fees/complaints and room vacancy.
9. **Testing, Limits, and Conclusion** - Test results, measured EXPLAIN plans, classroom-only authentication note, conclusion, and questions.
